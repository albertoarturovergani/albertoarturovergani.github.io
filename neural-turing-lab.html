What is simulated?

Two spatially organized populations of leaky integrate-and-fire neurons, one excitatory (E) and one inhibitory (I), with distinct recurrent coupling. Neurons emit discrete spikes when their membrane potential crosses threshold, then reset and enter a brief refractory period. Local excitation competes with longer-range inhibition, allowing structured firing-rate maps to arise.

How is the artwork made?

The displayed colors represent an exponentially smoothed trace of excitatory spikes, with a small spatial smoothing used only for display. No pre-rendered pattern is inserted. This is a simplified exploratory SNN with a periodic 2D topology; pattern appearance is parameter- and seed-dependent and should not be interpreted as a validated biological or mathematical Turing instability.

An experimental companion to the original Neural Turing Patterns artwork by Alberto Arturo Vergani, developed in collaboration with Laurent Perrinet. This prototype is distinct from the original simulation and artwork. Runs locally in your browser; no data is uploaded.
