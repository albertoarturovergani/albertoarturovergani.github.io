What is simulated?

A recurrent two-population E/I lattice is simulated with your choice of LIF, adaptive LIF (with spike-triggered recovery), or Izhikevich neurons (regular-spiking E and fast-spiking I). All three produce discrete spikes and feed the same spatial excitatory/inhibitory synaptic coupling. The central perturbation is an added Gaussian external input to excitatory neurons, with adjustable intensity, spread and pulse duration.

How is the artwork made?

The colors encode exponentially smoothed excitatory spike traces (blue = low, red = high), with mild display-only smoothing. The pulse marker is an overlay, while stimulation directly enters neuronal dynamics. Results depend on the model, coupling, stimulus history and random seed. This is an exploratory periodic-lattice SNN, not a validated biological model or proof of a mathematical Turing instability.
